Source files and metric definitions

Every percentage uses summed numerators and denominators. Estimated possessions average each team's FGA − OREB + turnovers + 0.44 × FTA; offensive/defensive ratings are points per 100 estimated possessions. Turnover rate uses those possessions; offensive rebound rate uses OREB / (OREB + opponent DREB). Minutes share is a player's minutes divided by all team player-minutes. A box-only roster label means the player appears in 2025–26 boxes but not the downloaded 2026 roster snapshot; it does not establish transfer, injury, or current eligibility. Numeric matchup ranks, where supplied, are user-provided context and are not verified NET rankings.
